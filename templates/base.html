🤓 The serious bit: this is a screening & self-discovery toy built on real instruments (PHQ-9, GAD-7, ASRS v1.1, AQ-10, MDQ, PC-PTSD-5, IPIP-50, DSM-5-TR traits, CHC-style cognitive tasks, EQ). It is not a diagnosis — flags are treasure marks for a conversation with a real clinical psychologist. 🗺️

🔒 How your data stays local

This site makes zero third-party requests. The font and charts are served from the site itself — nothing loads from Google, Cloudflare, or analytics trackers. No ads, no cookies for tracking, no pixel.
Your name stays in your browser. Your username and the name you type before screening exist only in a temporary session cookie in your browser. They are never written to disk and never leave your device.
What reaches the server (and is kept). Your scores, age band and gender are sent once, over TLS, to score your results and (only if you contribute them) to the anonymous community World View. Scores are stored without any name.
Raw answers are not saved. Your item-by-item answers are turned into scores during that single request, then discarded. What's kept is the anonymised score sheet on your “shelf” — never your name.
Your discussion guide never touches disk. The guide you download for your clinician is generated in the server's memory and deleted the moment it's fetched. Files are served from a device in the operator's home in the UAE, governed by UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021 (PDPA).
Audit it yourself. This project is open source. Anyone can read the few hundred lines of the scoring server and see exactly what happens to each form you submit. View the source →
