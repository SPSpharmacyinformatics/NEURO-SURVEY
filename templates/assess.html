What's actually in here?

Every instrument is a published clinical screening battery with real cutoffs: PHQ-9 (depression), GAD-7 (anxiety), ASRS v1.1 Part A (adult ADHD), AQ-10 (autism spectrum), MDQ (bipolar screen), PC-PTSD-5 (trauma), the IPIP-50 Big Five alongside DSM-5-TR PID-5 trait domains, a CHC-based cognitive battery (Gf, Gc, Gwm, Gv, Gq, Gs) and the Empathy Quotient. Scores are compared against published clinical thresholds. Only age, gender and scores are stored — never your name, email or identifying data. This is screening-level and not a medical diagnosis.
